Acoustics

The room is the other half of the sound

Bare walls and floors bounce sound around, so dialogue blurs and bass booms. We treat walls, ceilings and floors so you hear the film, not the room. Available on its own for theatres, studios and offices.

Walls

Fabric-wrapped panels on an aluminium frame, filled with 1000 GSM Recron and 18 mm wood wool. They absorb echo and look like part of the room.

Ceiling

Treated ceilings built on 19 mm plywood and HDHMR boards, with the Atmos speakers placed exactly where your seats need them.

Floor

14 mm carpet with an 1800 GSM pile that soaks up footsteps and reflections, and finishes the room in comfort.

How we work

From an empty room to opening night

  1. 01

    Site visit

    We measure the room, check the light and plan where the seating, screen and speakers go.

  2. 02

    Design & quotation

    You get a speaker layout and an itemised quotation for the package that suits the room.

  3. 03

    Installation

    Concealed wiring, in-wall and ceiling speakers, the screen and projector, and the acoustic work.

  4. 04

    Calibration

    We tune every speaker to your seats and the picture to your screen, then hand over the remote.
